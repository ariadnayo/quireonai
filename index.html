How Quireon works.

Four steps take a rough draft to a file you can print or upload.

  1. Upload your draft

    Add a Word file, a Google Doc or plain text. Quireon reads the whole manuscript, not just a page at a time.

  2. Review the edits

    The assistant flags unclear sentences, repeated words and inconsistent style. You accept or reject every change.

  3. Format the book

    Pick a trim size and a style. Quireon sets chapters, headings, margins and page numbers.

  4. Design the cover and export

    Choose from cover layouts built around your title. Export a print-ready PDF or an ebook file.
